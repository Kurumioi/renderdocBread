Keep the final answer brief and plain: changes, application state, which unpacked assets matched and were actually used, inferred roles and remaining unknowns. Mark confirmed, inferred and unknown meanings honestly: a known physical lane plus clear Shader use can support an inferred display field without a fully matched material. Source edits, retained material dictionaries and panel publication are separate effects; inspect the semantic-field publication counts and preserved/skipped rows. Resolve only that missing targeted work or document its concrete limitation; do not silently skip it. Skip repeated scans and unchanged adaptation, not unresolved preparation.
